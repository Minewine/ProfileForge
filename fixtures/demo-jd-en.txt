Harborline — Senior Backend Engineer
Permanent · London (hybrid, 2 days on-site) · Fintech analytics

About Harborline

Harborline builds the analytics platform that 400+ institutional treasury teams use to track
cash positions, FX exposure, and counterparty risk in real time. The backend engineering group
(18 people) owns the query layer, ingestion pipelines, and billing infrastructure.

What you will do

- Design and operate high-throughput backend services in TypeScript (Node.js) on AWS.
- Own PostgreSQL data modelling, query performance, and schema migrations.
- Evolve the GraphQL API surface together with the front-end team.
- Grow the platform: codify infrastructure with Terraform and tighten CI/CD.
- Review code, mentor engineers, and raise the bar on testing and observability.

What we are looking for

- 5+ years of professional experience building backend services with TypeScript.
- Production experience with PostgreSQL.
- Experience building and operating services on AWS.
- Fluent English, written and spoken.
- Experience with PCI DSS or financial compliance.

Nice to have

- Experience with GraphQL APIs at scale.
- Familiarity with Terraform or other infrastructure-as-code tools.
- Solid testing culture (Jest, contract tests).

Hiring process

Intro call (30 min), then a technical interview (60 min, systems + code), then a team
conversation and offer. We aim to complete the process within three weeks.

Compensation & benefits

£85,000 – £110,000 depending on experience, plus equity. Private healthcare, pension,
