Backend Engineer — Distributed Systems

You will join our platform group to design, build, and operate services in a fast-moving product organization. We value pragmatic engineering: strong fundamentals over buzzwords, measurable impact over vanity metrics.

What you will do:
- Build and evolve APIs in Go backed by PostgreSQL
- Participate in the design of our event-driven architecture (we use Kafka where it earns its complexity)
- Improve observability, latency, and reliability across the platform
- Review designs and mentor teammates

What we look for:
- Solid backend experience with Go or a similar statically typed language
- Comfort with relational data modeling and query performance
- Experience running services in production (Kubernetes or similar)
- Clear written communication; you can explain a trade-off in a paragraph

Nice to have: exposure to infrastructure-as-code (Terraform), caching layers (Redis), and bilingual environments.
